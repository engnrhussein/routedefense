# RouteDefense Group — Executive Business Card & Corporate Identity Package

State-of-the-art vector business cards, scannable QR codes, and corporate avatars for **RouteDefense Group** (`routedefensegroup.com`).

All assets are built in **100% pure resolution-independent SVG vector format**, with high-resolution $300\text{ DPI}$ print raster exports (`.jpg`).
